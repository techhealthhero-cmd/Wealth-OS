# Brand & Marketing artwork handoff

Codex creates and approves the artwork files in this folder. Claude handles
application integration and implementation QA.

## `splash-loading-v1.png`

- Purpose: splash screen, loading state, or opening transition artwork.
- Canvas: 1254 x 1254 PNG with real alpha transparency.
- Composition: closed forest-green leather journal, pencil, and a four-step
  journey arc. The object is centered with transparent padding.
- Keep the bitmap free of copy. Render product name, status text, and progress
  indicators as accessible UI text outside the image.
- Suggested use: contain-fit rather than crop; do not stretch or recolor.
- Check both light and dark backgrounds after integration.

## `feature-announcement-v1.png`

- Purpose: new-feature announcement card, release-note hero, or compact
  in-app announcement artwork.
- Canvas: 1254 x 1254 PNG with real alpha transparency.
- Composition: open forest-green journal, raised check card, restrained
  paper megaphone, sticky note, and binder clip.
- Keep the announcement title and supporting copy as accessible UI text.
  The gray rules in the artwork are decorative placeholders, not content.
- Suggested use: contain-fit with copy beside or above the image; do not crop
  the megaphone, journal rings, or colored section tabs.
- Check both light and dark backgrounds after integration.

## `product-preview-v1.png`

- Purpose: decorative product-preview art for landing sections, release
  notes, store screenshots, or marketing layouts.
- Canvas: 1254 x 1254 PNG with real alpha transparency.
- Composition: smartphone dashboard, ring-bound ledger, and calculator.
- The charts, colored dots, and gray rules are intentionally generic visual
  placeholders. Do not present them as a real customer's financial data.
- For an accurate product demonstration, place a real app screenshot beside
  this illustration rather than painting labels or balances into the PNG.
- Suggested use: contain-fit; keep the phone, ledger rings, and calculator
  fully visible. Check both light and dark backgrounds after integration.

## `social-share-v1.png`

- Purpose: social-share card base for financial progress, savings, and
  protection messages.
- Canvas: 1254 x 1254 PNG with alpha transparency outside the paper card.
- Composition: cream share card, gentle four-step progress path, protection
  shield, and savings jar. The upper half is intentionally copy-safe.
- Add the real share title, value, date, disclaimer, and brand mark as live
  template layers. Do not bake user data into this master PNG.
- Suggested use: keep the entire rounded paper card visible; avoid placing
  text below the chart line or behind the shield and jar.
- The rising path communicates gradual progress, not guaranteed returns.

## `landing-feature-journey-v1.png`

- Purpose: wide landing-page illustration for the Track -> Plan -> Earn ->
  Grow product story.
- Canvas: 1774 x 887 PNG (2:1) with real alpha transparency outside the map.
- Composition: receipt, calendar, work briefcase, and protected home connected
  by one calm four-checkpoint route across an unfolded paper map.
- Add the four stage labels and supporting copy as accessible HTML beside or
  below the artwork; do not paint labels into this PNG.
- Suggested use: contain-fit across the available width. Keep the receipt and
  protected home visible at both edges; on narrow screens, scale the complete
  map instead of center-cropping away a stage.
- The route shows a practical sequence, not guaranteed financial success.
